# Roadmap part: rendering and the far view (LoD)

Part of [`ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md). This part is the changelog of what is drawn and how it looks: the Photon port (G-buffer, sky, shadows, post), the 2026-10-20 lightweight frame that replaced it, textures, water, weather, light shafts, fog, and the Voxy-style voxel LoD (far terrain, its debug mode, its water). Speed and memory work is in [`performance.md`](performance.md). The LoD debug tool is described in [`LOD_DEBUG.md`](../LOD_DEBUG.md).

## Changelog

- 2026-10-25 (3): a lightning bolt lights the terrain (type-checked NOWHERE: no Rust toolchain in the session, nothing compiled, run or validated with naga / glslang; +1 test `effects::a_strike_lights_the_terrain_and_fades`; run `cargo test --lib effects look` and `cargo test --lib gles` (the uniform block is 192 bytes now) first). Until now a strike changed the sky and fog colour only (`sky::lightning_flash`); the ground stayed as dark as before the flash.
    - `Effects::light(partial)` = `[x, y + 6, z, k]` of the last bolt to strike (`struck_at`, kept while the flash fades after the bolt is gone), `k` = `(flash - partial)` clamped to 0..1, the same fade as the sky's. It goes `lib.rs` -> `look::Env.bolt` -> `Look.bolt` -> the new last vec4 of the chunk uniform block (`Uniforms.bolt`, WGSL and GLSL), so both renderers get it through `with_look` and no signature changed.
    - Shaders (`bolt_lit`, per vertex in `vert`, `vs_chunk`, `vs_chunk_water` and the GLSL `main`; real chunks, far terrain and water alike): `lit += (1 - lit) * k * (0.15 + 0.7 * f^2) * shade`, `f` = 1 - distance / 96 clamped. A floor of 15% everywhere (a flash lights the whole land, a far bolt included), up to 85% at the strike, and it pulls towards 1, so night and shadow go bright and a lit day surface barely moves. `shade` is the face shade, so a side face is a little darker than a top.
    - ponytail / not done: one light (the last strike; two bolts at once light round the later one); per vertex, so a merged 16 x 16 quad interpolates between its corners and a face turned away from the bolt is lit like one turned to it; one white scalar (`finish` still tints `lit` warm / cool by its value, so the flash reads slightly warm); mobs, items and the held hand are not lit (they take their own cell's light); no shadow from the bolt. Cost: one `length` and a few multiplies a vertex, nothing a pixel, and only while `k > 0` (the early return).
    - UNVERIFIED by eye: the 96 block range, the 15% floor and the 85% peak. If it washes the screen out lower the 0.7, if a far strike is not enough raise the 0.15 (both in the two `bolt_lit` copies; keep them equal).

- 2026-10-25: look polish: one Vibrance dial and five switches (type-checked on a Linux host and the shaders validated, not run on a device: all the numbers are taste values, judged by eye only; +8 tests in `render::look`, +1 in `options` and `world::atmos`, the `render::sky` fan test extended).

The dial scales all three: at 1.0 vibrance +0.5, curve 0.35, split 0.05; default 0.3 (+0.15 / 0.105 / 0.015). Costs one `if` and a few ALU in the pass that already runs, in Fast and Full alike.
    - 2 Warm light: no new varying. `finish()` multiplies the lit colour by `mix(cool, warm, smoothstep(0.15, 0.7, lit))`: bright faces take the warm tone, faces turned from the light (side, underside) and the shade go bluer by themselves, torch light stays warm. `warm` goes gold (1.10, 0.97, 0.76) with the sun low and dry (`Look::gold`, peak just above the horizon, gone by 45% up) and `cool` bluer then. The face shades get an extra exponent (1.10: `boost()` in `chunk_shade` and `flat_shade`, so the far terrain matches), the sides sink about 6%.
    - 3 Water and sky: `fs_water` (and the GLES water branch): the view angle scales the biome's water colour (upstream's `water_rgb`, entry 2026-10-24 (7)): lifted towards turquoise looking down, darker and bluer at a grazing look, so a swamp stays green and a jungle teal; a Blinn glint of the sun (the moon at 25%) on a slightly rippled surface, tops only, none in rain. ponytail: the view angle stands in for the real depth, the meshes carry none; the upgrade is the water column's depth per vertex (the vertex tint now carries the biome's colour, so it needs another field). Sky: `atmos::of` is the old table through `rich()`: the sky top +12% chroma and 3% deeper, the haze band +8%; the fog colour (the terrain's fade), clouds and stars untouched; edits saved by the player stay as they are.
    - 4 Glow: no bloom pass. `Scan` (one chunk a frame, round robin over 5 x 5 chunks, `ChunkManager::find_blocks`) keeps lava (surface cells, one per 4 blocks), torches, glowstone and flowers; `halos` draws the 36 nearest as camera-facing soft discs pulled half a block towards the eye, fading out between 24 and 40 blocks; the sun's halo sits at 92% of the far plane (terrain in front hides it) and goes gold when low. One new pipeline in each renderer (`ChunkPipeline::glow`, `Chunk::Glow`): the entity vertex, `fs_glow`, blend src alpha + one, depth-tested, not written, drawn after the water.
    - 5 Motion (Off / Low / On): leaves (tiles 53, 133, 255) sway 0.025 / 0.04 blocks in `vs_chunk` (a smooth function of the position, so corners stay joined); `Motes`: pollen above flowers by day, fireflies above water or flowers at night, at most 10 / 24 alive, 5 to 9 s each, one start every 0.35 s, within 14 blocks; light rays: 7 thin triangles out of the sun in `sky::fan_vertices` (now `fan_vertices(angle, rain, sun, rays)`, buffers `sky::FAN_MAX`), which only exists with the sun low, so the rays appear only then (alpha at most 0.16 of the glow).
    - 6 Haze: the fog factor brings a faint blue, brighter tint (`AER`) on the way to the fog colour (`finish()`), gone at both ends, so the far edge still meets the sky.
    - Options: Video Settings gets a 7th row, the `Vibrance` slider and `Effects...` (new `OptScr::Fx`: Warm Light, Water Look, Glow, Motion, Haze; Done goes back to Video). Saved as `look_vibrance`, `look_warm`, `look_water`, `look_glow`, `look_motion`, `look_haze` in `options.txt`; a bad value is skipped, the dial is clamped to 0..1.
    - Not done / limits: no real bloom of anything but the emitters (a sprite, not a blur); the water depth is the view angle; halos hide behind walls with the depth test only (no soft intersection); the sun glare of the sky quads is unchanged.
