## Rivers: what is still missing from Streams Reflowing 2.15.2

Source: `world/gen/hydro.rs`, a port read from the mod's bytecode (no source). Written WITHOUT a Rust toolchain: not compiled, not run, `hydro::tests` (README: the 16 older ones passed in a Linux scratch crate; `the_bed_is_deepest_on_the_centre_line` is new and not run); `tools/hydro_proto.py` ran. The mod is "All Rights Reserved": ask its author before publishing mc-rs with this port.
Labels: PORTED = read from the bytecode and followed; APPROX = something is there, but chosen here, not the mod's; MISSING = not there; N/A = cannot or should not be ported to b1.7.3 / this engine.
