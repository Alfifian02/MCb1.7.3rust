## Rivers: what is still missing from Streams Reflowing 2.15.2

Source: `world/gen/hydro.rs`, a port read from the mod's bytecode (no source). Written WITHOUT a Rust toolchain: not compiled, not run, 5 tests in `hydro::tests` not yet run; `tools/hydro_proto.py` ran. The mod is "All Rights Reserved": ask its author before publishing mc-rs with this port.
Labels: PORTED = read from the bytecode and followed; APPROX = something is there, but chosen here, not the mod's; MISSING = not there; N/A = cannot or should not be ported to b1.7.3 / this engine.

- 2026-10-19: rivers, streams and lakes for every world type, after the mod "Streams Reflowing" (written WITHOUT a Rust toolchain: not compiled, not run; +3 tests `hydro::tests`, `tools/hydro_proto.py` and `tools/biomes.py check` ran and passed). Replaces the valley rivers of the biome parameters (`Biome::River` and the river dip of `terrain()` are gone). New `world/gen/hydro.rs`: the terrain is read on a grid of one cell a chunk by the generator itself (`OverworldGenerator::probe`: the vanilla density column and its zero crossing, or `Tectonic::surface`, a new method of the crate), a region of 16 x 16 chunks plus 12 of margin is routed with a priority flood (every cell drains to the sea or the window's edge, depressions are filled), water volume (rain by humidity) is summed downstream and a cell past `STREAM` is a stream whose width grows with its volume. So the water runs downhill and **drains all the way to the sea**; a stream that leaves a region goes on in the next because a cell's direction depends only on its own neighbourhood. `Region::carve` (called by `generate_far`, so LoD tiles have it too) cuts the streams as smooth curves: a bed, banks that slope up from the waterline (cut into high ground, built up on low), by biome (gravel / sand beds, grass, sand in deserts, stone in mountains), flowers along the water, ice on top where cold; depressions of the filled terrain become lakes at their spill height, lower and sometimes dry in dry climates.
